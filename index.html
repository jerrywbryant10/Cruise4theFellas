March 2027 options

How to read it: Estimates assume full rooms (2 per balcony, 3 per 1-bedroom suite, 6 per 2-bedroom suite). The 3rd through 6th guests in a suite often pay less, so ask for the total suite price and divide by 6.
Carnival & MSC don't offer true 2-bedroom suites on these ships. Ask about connecting cabins or suites instead.
Other extras: shore excursions ($50–$150 each), specialty dining ($40–$75 pp per meal), casino, photos, and private-island cabanas ($300–$1,000+ split).
04 · Intel

Military discounts & deals

Ask for the military rate and the current promo together, because many lines let them stack. Bring your DD-214 (veterans) or military ID.

All lines: Wave Season (Jan–Mar) and Black Friday/Cyber Monday are the biggest sale windows. Book a refundable fare now and re-price if it drops. A travel agent can link all 3 cabins as a group and often adds onboard credit.
