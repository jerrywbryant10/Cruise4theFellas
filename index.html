04 · Intel

Military discounts & deals

Ask for the military rate and the current promo together, because many lines let them stack. Bring your DD-214 (veterans) or military ID.

All lines: Wave Season (Jan–Mar) and Black Friday/Cyber Monday are the biggest sale windows. Book a refundable fare now and re-price if it drops. A travel agent can link all 3 cabins as a group and often adds onboard credit.
